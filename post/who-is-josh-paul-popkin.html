Who Is Josh Paul Popkin?

Josh Paul Popkin is a New York-based founder, strategist and author working at the intersection of business growth, reputation and emerging technology.

November 12, 2026 · 1 min read

Josh Paul Popkin is a New York-based founder, strategist and author whose work focuses on business growth, reputation and the changing relationship between technology and how people make decisions.

Mr. Popkin is the founder of Position Strategy Group, a strategy, reputation and growth firm serving founder-led businesses and professional services organizations, including law firms, venture capital firms, homebuilders and multi-location operators.

The firm specializes in Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), an emerging field focused on how businesses are discovered, evaluated and recommended through artificial intelligence platforms such as ChatGPT, Google and Perplexity. Position Strategy Group works with business owners who have a strong sense of purpose and are committed to making a meaningful contribution through their work.

Before founding Position Strategy Group, Mr. Popkin co-founded an influencer marketing agency that worked on campaigns for some of the world's most recognizable brands, including McDonald's, Nike, Apple, Amazon, Meta and Google. Earlier in his career, he worked as a digital content creator.

Mr. Popkin is also the author of Play Offense, a book available on Amazon.

He holds a Master of Business Administration from Cornell University, where he was named a Cornell Tech Merit Scholar in 2025. He earned his Bachelor of Science from Bucknell University, studying Markets, Innovation & Design at the Freeman College of Management.

Originally from Westport, Connecticut, Mr. Popkin currently lives in New York City.
